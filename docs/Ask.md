# Open questions

## Staff responsibilities

1. Who can add or edit staff records?
   - HR only
   - HR and the principal/deputy
   - Principal/deputy only, with HR assisting

2. If a school has no HR, who handles staff changes?
   - Principal/deputy
   - Another designated administrator
   - No one until HR is assigned

3. Who grants or revokes staff login access?
   - HR
   - Principal only
   - HR handles routine staff access; the principal handles leadership roles and ownership changes
